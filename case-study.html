Case Study 1: U.S. Residential Real Estate Operations (California)

Residential Real Estate Operations & VA California Market

The Challenge

Leads, active transaction contracts, marketing campaigns, and daily listing administration lacked centralized tracking and standardized follow-through.

The Approach

Built centralized CRM tracking systems, standardized C.A.R./ZipForm transaction workflows, maintained CRMLS listing data, and executed consistent marketing campaigns.

CRM & Transaction Support

  • Follow Up Boss & Century 21 database upkeep
  • ZipForm contract preparation
  • DocuSign coordination
  • RentSpree lease documentation

Marketing & Social Media

  • TikTok growth
  • Instagram content scheduling
  • Open House & Just Sold campaigns
  • Zillow Premier Agent review management

The Result

"Created a centralized operational framework tracking 72+ active contracts, maintaining a 5.0 ★ Zillow Premier Agent profile rating, and allowing the agent to focus 100% on client acquisition and closing deals."

Case Study 2: Real Estate Media QA & Post-Production (Oregon)

Real Estate Media Quality Control & 3D Tours Oregon MLS Market

The Challenge

High-volume real estate photography packages required rigorous quality inspection, rapid revision turnaround, floor plan generation, and multi-platform media delivery without delaying property listing launches.

The Approach

Established a multi-point QA inspection pipeline for photo packages, executed minor retouching in Lightroom/Photoshop, generated CubiCasa schematic floor plans, produced Matterport & Zillow 3D Home virtual tours, and edited property videos in Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve.

Media QA/QC & Retouching

  • Multi-point photo inspection (color balance, composition, missing shots)
  • Minor Lightroom/Photoshop corrections
  • External editing team coordination

Virtual Tours & Video Production

  • CubiCasa floor plan processing
  • Matterport and Zillow 3D Home walkthrough builds
  • Cinematic listing video editing via Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve

The Result

"Achieved 100% on-time media package delivery across Oregon MLS listings with zero post-delivery quality errors, streamlining agent marketing launches."
